# About Flipside

By day, you move between emails and meetings. You introduce yourself with the title on your business card. By night, you practice guitar, play ball, or log on under an ID that only fellow fans would know.

Both are the real you. Yet they rarely share the same space.

Most personal websites have one layout and one way to tell your story. That forces a choice: show your profession, or share your passion? In the end, one side usually has to stand for all of you.

But why choose?

Think of a coin. Profession is on the front and passion is on the back. Both faces are the real you. One gentle flip lets visitors see you from a very different angle and know you better.

This is "Flipside", an Astro theme with a clean design and good care for zh-TW typography. It grew out of the author's personal website. The private content was removed, and the ideas gathered along the way became a ready-to-use starting point. It helps more people build a space of their own that shows their professional strength and also lets their personality through.
